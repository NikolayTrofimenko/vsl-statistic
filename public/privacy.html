Euphoria / VSL Statistic

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

EUPHORIA VSL Statistic ("the app", available at https://trueeuphoriaanalytics.online) is an internal tool used only by the EUPHORIA team. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and how it can be removed.

1. Google user data

When a team member connects Google Drive, the app requests the drive.readonly scope plus the basic openid and email scopes. With them the app:

  • reads the list of video files inside one Drive folder chosen by the team: file name, size, duration, resolution, modification date, folder path and link;
  • downloads a preview image (thumbnail) for each of those videos;
  • streams a video to a signed-in team member when they press play — the video is passed through and not stored;
  • reads the email address of the connected Google account to show which account is connected.

The app does not create, modify, share or delete any files in Google Drive and does not access files outside the configured folder.

2. How Google user data is stored and protected

  • File metadata and thumbnails are stored in the app's database on the team's own server.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Access tokens are kept only in memory.
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS. The app interface is protected by a team password.

3. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models. It is used solely to provide the video library feature to the EUPHORIA team.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Landing page visitors

On the team's landing pages a script measures video playback: which seconds of the video were played, whether a form was submitted, the device type and browser family, the referring site, and campaign parameters from the page URL. IP addresses are not stored, and no names, phone numbers or other contact details are collected by this script.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting Google Drive in the app deletes the stored refresh token and revokes it at Google.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • File metadata and thumbnails are kept while the files remain in the folder; to delete them, contact us at the address below.

EUPHORIA VSL Statistic

An internal analytics tool of the EUPHORIA team. It is not a public service: access is limited to team members with a password.

What it does

  • Video library. Lists the sales videos (VSL) stored in the team's own Google Drive folder, shows their previews and duration, and lets the team group them and watch them in one place.
  • Video quality on landing pages. Measures how long visitors of the team's landing pages actually watch those videos, so the team can see where viewers drop off and which video works better.

Google Drive access

The app requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly) and only reads the single folder the team configured. It never creates, changes, shares or deletes anything in Drive. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
